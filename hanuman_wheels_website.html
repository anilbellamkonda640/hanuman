Expert Services

Complete Wheel & Tyre Care Solutions

Equipped with state-of-the-art computerized machinery for maximum precision, vehicle longevity, and a smooth ride.

Wheel Balancing

Eliminates steering vibrations and uneven tread wear by counterbalancing weight imbalances in your wheel assembly using high-precision digital sensors.

  • Smooth steering at high speeds
  • Extends tyre lifespan

Wheel Alignment

Laser-guided 3D wheel alignment ensures your vehicle tracks straight, improves fuel economy, and prevents your car or bike from pulling to one side.

  • Precision 3D laser alignment
  • Better fuel efficiency

Tyre Fitting

Professional mounting and demounting for cars, bikes, and scootys without damaging alloy wheels or rims, using modern pneumatic changers.

  • Scratch-free rim care
  • Valve replacement included

Nitrogen / Gas Filling

Maintain consistent tyre pressure across varying temperatures with clean nitrogen gas filling. Reduces pressure loss and improves tyre safety.

  • Stable tyre pressure
  • Cooler running tyres
